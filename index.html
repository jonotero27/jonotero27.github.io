Otero Shop Assistant

A personal tool for managing my own Etsy shop.

What it does

  • Researches product trends using public Etsy listing data
  • Helps me create and manage listings in my shop (every new product is reviewed and approved by me before it goes live)
  • Reads my shop's orders to track sales, costs, and profit

This app is used only by the shop owner, for a single shop. It is not offered to other sellers or to the public.

Privacy policy

  • Data accessed: my own shop's listings, orders, and receipts, plus public listing data from the Etsy API.
  • Buyer information: used only to fulfill orders and calculate shop totals. It is never sold, shared, or used for marketing outside Etsy.
  • Storage: data is stored privately on my own devices and accounts, kept only as long as needed for bookkeeping, and deleted on request.
  • Third parties: order details are shared only with my print-on-demand production partner as needed to make and ship orders.
